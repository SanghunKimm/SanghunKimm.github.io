문제
\begin{problem}
Let V and W be vector spaces such that dim(V) = dim(W), 
and let T : V $\to$ W be linear. Show that there exist ordered bases $\beta$ and $\gamma$ for V and W, respectively, such that $[T]_{\beta}^{\gamma}$ is a diagonal matrix.
\end{problem}
