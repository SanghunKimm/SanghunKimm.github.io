### Problem 3.2.21

Let $$A$$ be an $$m \times n$$ matrix with $$\mathrm{rank}(A) = m$$.  
Prove that there exists an $$n \times m$$ matrix $$B$$ such that $$AB = I_m$$.


### Solution 

Let the columns of $$B$$ be denoted by $$b_j$$. Then:

$$
AB = I_m \Longleftrightarrow A b_j = e_j \quad \text{for all } j = 1, \ldots, m.
$$

Without loss of generality, fix $$j = 1$$.  
Let $$A'$$ be the $$(m - 1) \times n$$ matrix obtained by removing the first row of $$A$$.  
Then:

$$
A' b_1 = \mathbf{0} \quad \Rightarrow \quad b_1 \in N(A').
$$

Note that:

$$
\mathrm{nullity}(A') = n - (m - 1) = n - m + 1 \geq 1,
$$

while:

$$
\mathrm{nullity}(A) = n - m.
$$

Since we want $$A b_1 = e_1$$, it suffices to show that $$b_1 \notin N(A)$$.  
This is easily verified because:

$$
N(A) \leq N(A') \quad \text{and} \quad \mathrm{nullity}(A) = n - m < \mathrm{nullity}(A') = n - m + 1.
$$

Therefore, there exists $$b_1 \in N(A') \setminus N(A)$$ such that:

$$
A' b_1 = \mathbf{0}, \quad \text{but} \quad A b_1 = e_1.
$$

By scalar multiplication and the same reasoning for each $$j$$, we can construct column vectors $$b_j$$ satisfying $$A b_j = e_j$$.  
Thus, we obtain a matrix $$B$$ such that:

$$
AB = I_m \quad \blacksquare
$$

### Problem 3.2.22

Let $$B$$ be an $$n \times m$$ matrix with $$\mathrm{rank}(B) = m$$.  
Prove that there exists an $$m \times n$$ matrix $$A$$ such that $$AB = I_m$$.

### Solution

Note that:

$$
AB = I_m \Longleftrightarrow B^T A^T = I_m.
$$

Now, since $$B^T$$ is an $$m \times n$$ matrix with

$$
\mathrm{rank}(B^T) = \mathrm{rank}(B) = m,
$$

this reduces to the same problem as in Exercise 3.2.21, which we have already shown $$\blacksquare$$

### Problem 3.2.20

Let 

$$
A =
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 & -1 & 2 & 1 \\
-1 & 1 & 3 & -1 & 0 \\
-2 & 1 & 4 & -1 & 3 \\
3 & -1 & -5 & 1 & -6
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

#### (a)

Find a $$5 \times 5$$ matrix $$M$$ with rank $2$ such that $$AM = O$$,  
where $$O$$ is the $$4 \times 5$$ zero matrix.

#### (b)

Suppose that $$B$$ is a $$5 \times 5$$ matrix such that $$AB = O$$.  
Prove that $$\mathrm{rank}(B) \leq 2$$.

* * * 

이 문제에서 (a)를 풀다 보면, 행렬 M을 일차독립인 두 열벡터와, 그 열벡터들의 선형결합인 나머지 세 열벡터로 만들어야겠다는 생각이 듭니다.
그래면 A와 M의 각 열벡터의 곱이 영벡터여야 하므로, M의 열벡터가 A의 N(A)의 원소여야 하는데, 여기서 차원정리에 의해 Nullity가 2가 됨을 알고, 이를 통해 
**M의 열벡터 중 일차독립인 건 최대 두개뿐이다** 를 알 수 있습니다. 그러면 (b)도 자연스럽게 증명됩니다.
위 아이디어를 이용하여 3.2.21도 풀이하였는데, 다른 접근이 있다면 알려주시면 감사하겠습니다.
